Criminal emergencies · 24/7

If it is happening now, call.

In an arrest or a search, every hour counts. While we get in touch, these are the basic rules.

A family member was arrested

  1. Ask who is making the arrest (Police, CTI), for what offense and where the person is being taken.
  2. Remind them that they have the right to remain silent and to a lawyer. They must not make statements about the facts without their lawyer.
  3. Write down the time of the arrest: the person must be brought before a judge within 36 hours at the latest.
  4. Gather their ID, proof of roots in the community (home, job, family) and any medical information.
  5. Call us. We locate the person, meet with them and prepare the hearings.

Search of a home or business

  1. Stay calm and do not physically resist the search.
  2. Ask for the order and read it: it must state the place and the purpose of the search.
  3. Call your lawyer and ask that the call be recorded in the report.
  4. Do not answer questions about the facts. Just identify yourself.
  5. Read the report (acta) before signing it and ask for a copy. Write down what was taken.

Summons from the Prosecutor's Office or the Judicial Police

  1. Do not ignore it, but do not attend without knowing why you are being summoned.
  2. Check the case number, the office and the capacity in which you are summoned: witness, victim or suspect.
  3. Do not give explanations over the phone or «informal versions».
  4. Consult a lawyer before the date: it is possible to request a postponement and access to the available information.
